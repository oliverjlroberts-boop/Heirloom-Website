1. About Heirloom

Heirloom is a life story service that captures a loved one's memories through guided voice interviews and transforms them into a beautifully written memoir. The service is operated by Ollie as a sole trader based in the United Kingdom.

2. The Service

When you purchase Heirloom, you are purchasing a guided life story interview experience for a named individual (the "narrator") and the creation of a written memoir based on those interviews. Depending on the package selected, the deliverable is a digital PDF memoir, a printed book, or both.

The interview is conducted over Zoom and typically takes place across several sessions of 30 to 60 minutes each, scheduled at times convenient to the narrator.

3. Pricing and Payment

All prices are listed on our website in pounds sterling and include UK delivery where applicable. Payment is taken at the time of purchase via Stripe. We reserve the right to change our prices at any time, but this will not affect orders already placed.

4. What You Receive

Digital package: a professionally written memoir delivered as a PDF, plus a copy of the audio recordings from the interview sessions.

Digital + Book package: everything in the digital package, plus one printed memoir book delivered to a UK address.

Family Bundle: everything in the digital + book package, but for two narrators, with two printed copies.

Delivery timescales vary depending on scheduling and are agreed with you directly. We aim to deliver the completed memoir within 8 weeks of the final interview session.
